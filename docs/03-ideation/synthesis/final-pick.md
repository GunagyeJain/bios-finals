# Final pick

## Chosen idea
**EgoVision Data Engine**: PS5 built on PS1. Robust laptop hand tracking + a Data Health light (🟢🟡🔴 with reasons) on every second of footage, feeding a **robot-ready dataset export** (hero), then the brief's other uses: step-order checks, Work Module training clips, safety alerts. Spec: `../../05-solution/prd.md`.

## Parts merged in from other ideas
- Bhavyam: Data Health light, hand-fix list (H1–H11), A–B–C business model, Work Modules, eval plan.
- Abhijay: robotics buyers as primary goal, robot-ready export, per-clip datasheet, redaction.
- Shefali: grip-type tags (2-finger / 5-finger / generic) as P2 export filter; "grade footage, never people".

## Why this one
All three plans converged on hand data for robot learning; the light makes hand accuracy (30 pts + tiebreak) and usefulness one story, and runs on a laptop.

## What we drop
- Shefali's real-vs-faked-work rhythm scoring (judges worker behaviour; no labels to prove it).
- PS4 3D world-frame motion (WiLoR 3D joints go into the export only).
- Live processing on stage.
